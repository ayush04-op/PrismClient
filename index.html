Telegram, on paper.

Prism is a calmer Android client for Telegram: warm paper colours, serif type, and small tools that stay out of your way.

Download for Android Source code

Free and open source. Unofficial client, uses the Telegram API, not affiliated with Telegram.

One app, six kinds of paper.

Scroll to see what changes. Everything on the phone is drawn live, the same way Prism draws it.

It starts on Paper.

Warm white pages, ink-brown text and a terracotta accent. Easy on the eyes for long chats.

Pick another paper whenever you like.

Sage, Ocean, Plum, Amber and Slate. Every screen follows your pick, from the chat list to settings.

Each paper has a dark side.

Follow your phone's dark mode, switch on a schedule you set, or choose light or dark yourself.

Lock the chats that are yours alone.

A locked chat opens with your fingerprint. Its preview stays hidden in the chat list and in notifications.

Give a chat its own colour.

Tint one chat with any palette so it stands out, without changing the rest of the app.

Updates inside the app
Prism tells you when a new build is out and installs it for you.

Install in three steps.

  1. Download the APK

    Get the newest build from the releases page on your phone.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Allow it, then tap Install.

  3. Open Prism

    A short tour lets you pick a paper. Then log in with your Telegram account as usual.

Questions

Is Prism made by Telegram?

No. Prism is an unofficial client built on the open-source Telegram for Android app. It uses the Telegram API and isn't affiliated with Telegram.

Can I keep the Telegram app as well?

Yes. Prism installs as a separate app, so you can have both and log in to the same account in each.

Does Prism follow Telegram's rules for apps?

Prism uses its own API ID and leaves out features Telegram's API terms don't allow, such as hiding read receipts or your online status. Sponsored messages show as Telegram requires.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer on a 64-bit (arm64) phone, which covers most phones from the last several years. Liquid glass needs Android 13 or newer.

How do updates work?

Prism checks this project's GitHub releases and offers new builds inside the app. You can also download them yourself from the releases page.

Does it cost anything?

No. Prism is free, and all of its code is public on GitHub.

Your chats stay between you and Telegram.

Prism has no servers of its own.

  • Messages go directly between your phone and Telegram.
  • Your settings stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up.
  • Prism contacts GitHub only to check for updates.
  • The AI assistant is off until you add your own key, and then it talks only to the provider you chose.
  • Every line of code is public on GitHub.
