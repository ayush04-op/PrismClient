Telegram client for Android

A calmer way to use Telegram.

Prism wraps your chats in warm paper colours and a clean serif type, with useful tools that stay out of your way.

Download the APK View on GitHub

Free and open source. Unofficial client. Uses the Telegram API. Not affiliated with Telegram.

Pick your look

Six paper palettes, each with a light and a dark side. Try them here. The app works the same way.

Small tools, done well

Everything is built into Prism and styled to match your palette.

Chat colours

Give any chat its own accent, so your important chats are easy to tell apart.

Locked chats

Lock a chat with your fingerprint. Its previews are hidden in the chat list and in notifications.

Link and file finder

Search links, files and photos across all your chats at once, then jump straight to the message.

Home-screen widget

See your unread count at a glance, in light or dark to match your phone.

Install in three steps

  1. Download

    Get the latest APK from the releases page.

  2. Allow the install

    Android will ask once to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Say yes.

  3. Open and log in

    Open Prism, pick a look and log in with your Telegram account. Later updates arrive inside the app.

Your privacy

Prism has no servers of its own.

  • Your chats go directly between your phone and Telegram.
  • Your settings stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up.
  • Prism contacts GitHub only to check for updates.
  • The AI assistant is off until you add your own key, and then it talks only to the provider you chose.
  • All of the code is public on GitHub.
